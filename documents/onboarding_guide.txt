TITLE: New Employee Onboarding Guide
DOCUMENT ID: onboarding-001
CATEGORY: Onboarding

==============================================================
WEEK 1: YOUR FIRST WEEK
==============================================================

Day 1 Checklist
- Collect your access badge from reception (Building A, Ground Floor) between 9–10 AM.
- Meet your onboarding buddy — assigned by HR before your start date.
- Complete IT setup: laptop provisioning takes ~2 hours; bring your employee ID.
- Attend the company orientation session at 2 PM in the Main Conference Room.
- Set up corporate email, Slack, and the HR portal (credentials emailed to personal address).

Day 2–3: Systems Access
- Request access to required tools via the IT Helpdesk portal: it.company.internal
- Standard tools provisioned automatically: Slack, Zoom, Jira, Confluence, GitHub.
- Role-specific tools (Salesforce, AWS, Figma, etc.) require manager approval — submit ticket within your first week.

Day 4–5: Team Integration
- Schedule 1:1 introductory meetings with all direct teammates.
- Review the team charter and current sprint goals in Confluence.
- Attend your first team standup — ask your manager for the recurring invite link.

==============================================================
WEEK 2–4: RAMP-UP PERIOD
==============================================================

Training Requirements (mandatory, complete within 30 days)
1. Information Security Awareness — 45 min (LMS portal)
2. Data Privacy & GDPR Basics — 30 min (LMS portal)
3. Code of Conduct & Ethics — 20 min (LMS portal)
4. Product Walkthrough — schedule with Product team
5. Customer Journey Overview — schedule with Solutions team

Key Systems to Learn
- HRIS (Workday): payroll, leave, and personal information management.
- Expense Reporting (Concur): submit all expenses within 30 days of incurring.
- Project Tracking (Jira): your primary day-to-day work tracker.
- Documentation (Confluence): team wikis, runbooks, and process documentation.
- Internal Chat (Slack): join #general, #engineering, #announcements, and your team channel.

==============================================================
PROBATION PERIOD
==============================================================

- The standard probation period is 90 days.
- Your manager will conduct a 30-day and 90-day check-in.
- Objectives for your first 90 days should be set with your manager by the end of Week 1.
- Successful completion of probation is confirmed via a written notification from HR.
