TITLE: Employee HR Policy & Benefits Guide
DOCUMENT ID: hr-policy-001
CATEGORY: Human Resources

==============================================================
SECTION 1: LEAVE POLICY
==============================================================

Annual Leave
- Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
- Leave accrues at 1.67 days per month from the date of joining.
- A maximum of 5 unused days may be carried forward to the next calendar year.
- Leave requests must be submitted at least 7 days in advance through the HR portal.

Sick Leave
- Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
- A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.
- Sick leave does not carry forward to the following year.

Parental Leave
- Primary caregiver: 16 weeks paid parental leave.
- Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks paid parental leave.
- Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

==============================================================
SECTION 2: HEALTH & WELLNESS BENEFITS
==============================================================

Medical Insurance
- All employees are covered under the company group health plan from Day 1.
- Plan covers: hospitalization, outpatient, dental, and vision.
- Dependents (spouse and up to 2 children) can be added at subsidized rates.

Mental Health Support
- Free access to the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) — 8 counseling sessions per year.
- Wellness reimbursement: up to $500/year for gym, yoga, or wellness apps.

==============================================================
SECTION 3: COMPENSATION & PAYROLL
==============================================================

Salary Cycle
- Salaries are processed on the last business day of each month.
- Payslips are available via the HR portal by the 25th of each month.

Performance Reviews
- Annual performance reviews are conducted in December.
- Mid-year check-ins are held in June.
- Merit increases take effect from January 1 of the following year.

==============================================================
SECTION 4: CODE OF CONDUCT
==============================================================

- All employees must complete the annual compliance training by March 31.
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed to the HR department immediately.
- The company has a zero-tolerance policy for harassment and discrimination.
- Violations should be reported via the confidential ethics hotline: ethics@company.internal

==============================================================
SECTION 5: REMOTE WORK POLICY
==============================================================

Hybrid Model
- Employees are expected to be in the office a minimum of 3 days per week (Tuesday–Thursday).
- Remote work on Mondays and Fridays is permitted by default.
- Full remote arrangements require manager and HR approval.

Equipment
- The company provides a laptop, monitor, and peripherals for home office use.
- A one-time home office stipend of $400 is available for ergonomic accessories.
